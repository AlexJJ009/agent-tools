# Academic Writing

Identify the requested genre, audience, section, language, evidence packet, and output target. Work directly in the manuscript project's authorized files when asked, or return prose in conversation. Source facts from supplied material or verified references. Mark unsupported claims and missing results; do not invent citations, experiments, effect sizes, or novelty. A conditional proposal or argument outline is useful when evidence is incomplete, but label its assumptions and falsification criterion.

## Modes

- **Draft:** Write the requested whole or partial manuscript, abstract, proposal alternatives, or response text. Make each rhetorical move serve its reader: establish the problem, relevant gap, contribution, evidence, and qualification as the genre requires. CARS (Create a Research Space) can guide an introduction when it actually fits; it is not a fixed template.
- **Revise:** Locate changes to paragraphs or sentences, preserve the author's intended claim, and explain material edits. Use a reverse outline to inspect paragraph purpose and transitions when the structure is unclear. Return a clean draft or targeted diff as requested.
- **Argument review:** Map each consequential claim to evidence and the warrant connecting them. State a qualifier, alternative explanation, or missing observation where the inference outruns the evidence. For related work, use a synthesis matrix around comparable conditions, then position the contribution without equating an unsuccessful search with novelty proof.
- **Teaching recipe:** When the user asks to practice, analyze a model passage, give a bounded writing attempt, preserve the learner's original text, and offer located feedback. Track how much help was supplied. A worked example may precede a faded attempt or follow the learner's attempt; provide a full example immediately when requested. Do not infer mastery from an AI rewrite.

Direct draft, revise, and argument review do not require prerequisites, retrieval checks, or a teaching manifest. When the current activity is guided learning, coordinate with `teaching-reconstruction` and `retrieval-practice` only for the requested exercise. Keep answer text separate from an exercise prompt until the appropriate stage.

Read the packaged [reader-facing contract](../work-report/references/writing-contract.md) for W1–W9 expression principles when available. Those principles adapt to manuscript and teaching genres; Work Report's six sections and Judge do not apply. Normative instructions remain English; the deliverable follows the requested language and genre.

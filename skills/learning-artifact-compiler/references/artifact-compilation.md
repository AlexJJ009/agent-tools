# Artifact Compilation Notes

Compile only from verified or explicitly provisional session state.

## Patterns

- Tutorial: prerequisite map, mechanism, worked example, checks.
- Blog: hook, reader promise, reconstruction, evidence, caveats.
- Study note: compact KC graph, anchors, and spaced review prompts.
- Audit memo: claim table, evidence strength, gaps, and next checks.

Keep uncertainty visible. Do not upgrade provisional evidence during prose polishing.

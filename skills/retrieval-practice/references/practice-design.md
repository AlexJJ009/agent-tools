# Practice Design Notes

Good checks force reconstruction.

## Check types

- Reconstruction: rebuild the mechanism or proof path from memory.
- Near transfer: apply the same idea with surface changes.
- Far transfer: choose whether the idea applies in a different setting.
- Audit: find a weak claim, missing prerequisite, or over-generalization.

## Scoring

Score for mechanism, boundary, and evidence use. Do not require exact wording.
